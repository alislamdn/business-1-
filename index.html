What kinds of websites do you build?

Who do you work with?

We work with Muslim communities, businesses, organizations, and people with an idea for a useful website or digital project.

Can you update a website that already exists?

Yes. Share your current site and the changes you're looking for so we can discuss the scope and a suitable approach.

How do you handle Quran text and translations?

Content, translation, and source requirements should be agreed on for each project. Religious text and translations should be confirmed against sources you trust before publication.

Will my website work on phones?

Websites are designed to adapt to phone, tablet, and desktop screens.
